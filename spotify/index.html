Connect Spotify for Lyrics

  1. Please make sure you pay for Spotify Premium. This will not work for free users.

  2. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.

  3. Tap Create app and fill out the following details:

    • App namemooboard
    • App descriptionMy mooboard
    • Redirect URIhttps://api.mooboard.co/link/spotify/callbackThen tap Add.
    • Under APIs used, check Web API.
    • Agree to the terms and tap Save.

  4. Open Settings, then User Management. Add your name and the email of your Spotify account, then tap Save.

  5. Open Settings, then Basic Information, and copy the Client ID.

  6. Close this page and paste the Client ID in your board’s setup page. Then tap Agree on Spotify’s page.

Spotify asks you to sign in again every 6 months. Your board will tell you when.
