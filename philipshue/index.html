Set Up Philips Hue

You need a Hue Bridge and color Hue lights.

Pair your bridge

  1. Open the Lights app in your mooboard’s web app.

  2. Press the round link button on top of your Hue Bridge.

  3. Within 30 seconds, tap Pair with bridge.

Why use an entertainment area

An entertainment area is a group of Hue lights the bridge can change all at once, many times a second. Hue uses it to sync lights with movies, games, and music.

With one, Disco streams color to every light in the area about 25 times a second. Without one, mooboard sends changes one at a time, and the bridge only takes about 10 a second, so the lights lag and look choppy.

Make an entertainment area

  1. Open the Hue app and go to Settings, then Entertainment areas.

  2. Add an area and pick the room your mooboard is in.

  3. Choose the lights you want to dance along.

  4. Place them where they sit in the room, then save.

Turn it on in mooboard

  1. In the Lights app, turn on Use entertainment area.

  2. Pick your area. If you only have one, mooboard picks it for you.

Only color Hue lights can join an entertainment area.
