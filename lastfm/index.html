Connect Last.fm for Scrobbles

  1. Sign in to Last.fm, or make a free account.

  2. Open last.fm/api/account/create. Fill in an application name, like “mooboard”, and your email. The rest can stay empty.

  3. Copy the API key it shows. Not the shared secret.

  4. In the mooboard web app, open Media, turn on Last.fm, and enter your Last.fm username and the API key. Then tap Save.

Your music app must scrobble to Last.fm for the board to see it.
