These terms cover the $1 VIP pass for mooboard one. By buying one, you agree to them.

What you get

  • One VIP pass, which can only be used for one mooboard one.

How you get the discount

The $1

Dates

  • Passes expire if they are not used by January 31, 2027. The coupon or private link expires then too.
  • We’ll email you the launch date before anyone else.

Buying

  • At launch, you pay the price, shipping, and any tax when you buy. Shipping and taxes are not included in the price.
  • We ship in the US first.

Payments and your details

  • Stripe handles every payment. We never see or keep your card details.
