Mounting

    Screws

    1. Hold the rail level on the wall.
    2. Mark the wall through the rail’s 3 holes.
    3. Take the rail down and screw an anchor into each mark.
    4. Screw the rail on.
    5. Line up the hooks with the rail’s round holes and push in.
    6. Lift the board a little and slide it left until it clicks.

    Adhesive strips

    1. Press a strip into each of the rail’s 4 pockets.
    2. Hold the rail level on a clean wall and press it on.
    3. Wait 1 hour before you hang the board.
    4. Line up the hooks with the rail’s round holes and push in.
    5. Lift the board a little and slide it left until it clicks.

    Join its Wi-Fi

    After plugging in your mooboard, it will show info to join its Wi-Fi.

    • Wi-Fi SSID: mooboard-XXXX
    • Password: See on board.

    You can also scan the QR code with your phone’s camera.

    Once connected, a captive portal will launch where you will input your home’s Wi-Fi details.

    If it doesn’t open, go to http://4.3.2.1 in your browser manually.

    Note: mooboard can only join WPA2, WPA3 Personal or open networks on the 2.4GHz band. Press here for more details.

      Wi-Fi help

      Tips

      • mooboard needs a 2.4 GHz network. Most routers broadcast one under the same name as the rest. It may also be its own, like Home_2.4.
      • It joins WPA2 and WPA3 Personal networks, and open ones. Not networks that ask for a username.
      • Do not pick a guest network. Wi-Fi with a sign-in page, like at hotels, schools, and work, won’t work.
      • A VPN or iCloud Private Relay can hide that you are home. Turn it off for your home Wi-Fi, so mooboard.co/hi can find the board.
      • Can’t see your network? Move the board closer to your router and scan again.
      • A hidden network? Tap Other network and type its name.

      Change Wi-Fi later

      • In the web app, open System, then Board. On Wi-Fi Status, tap Change Wi-Fi.
      • Pick the network, type its password and tap Save and restart.
      • Then join your phone to your home Wi-Fi and open mooboard.co/portal.
      • If the new network fails, the board shows its code again after 2 minutes.

      Factory reset

      • Hold for 10 seconds.
      • Then the board restarts and shows its code to join.
      • Or in the web app, open System, then Advanced. Under Erase everything, type RESET and tap Erase.
