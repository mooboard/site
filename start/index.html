Mounting

    Adhesive strips

    1. Press a strip into each of the rail’s 4 pockets.
    2. Hold the rail level on a clean wall and press it on.
    3. Wait 1 hour before you hang the board.
    4. Line up the hooks with the rail’s round holes and push in.
    5. Lift the board a little and slide it left until it clicks.

    Screws

    1. Hold the rail level on the wall.
    2. Mark the wall through the rail’s 3 holes.
    3. Take the rail down. Screw an anchor into each mark.
    4. Screw the rail on.
    5. Line up the hooks with the rail’s round holes and push in.
    6. Lift the board a little and slide it left until it clicks.

    Join its Wi-Fi

    Plug it in. Its screen shows how to join its own Wi-Fi.

    • A code to scan and Scan to set up.
    • Its Wi-Fi name, mooboard-XXXX.
    • PW and its password, 8 digits.
    • The status light breathes blue while it waits.

    Scan the code with your phone’s camera, or join mooboard-XXXX with the password shown.

    Scan the code

    • iPhone: open the Camera and point it at the code. Tap the Wi-Fi banner, then Join.
    • Android: open the Camera or Google Lens and point it at the code. Tap to join the network.

    Or join it by hand

    • iPhone: open Settings, then Wi-Fi. Pick mooboard-XXXX and type the 8 digits after PW.
    • Android: open Settings, then your Wi-Fi list. Pick mooboard-XXXX and type the 8 digits after PW.

    Already set up? Open mooboard.co/portal on the same Wi-Fi as your mooboard.

    Set up

    The setup page opens. Pick your home Wi-Fi.

    • If it doesn’t open, go to http://4.3.2.1 in your browser.
    • Type its password and tap Join. It can take up to a minute.

    On the board

    • Setting up Wi-FiYour phone has the setup page open.
    • Joiningyour networkIt is joining your home Wi-Fi.
    • Connectedmooboard.co/hiIt joined your Wi-Fi.
    • Wrong passwordtry again.For 10 seconds. Then it shows its code again.

    If it can’t join

    The page says why, and the board shows its code again. The page can say:

    • That password did not work. Check it and try again.
    • mooboard could not find your network. It needs a 2.4 GHz network, close enough to hear.
    • This network is incompatible. Please join a WPA2 or WPA3 Personal network on 2.4 GHz.
    • mooboard could not join your network. Check the router is on, then try again.

    After it joins

    • The board restarts onto your Wi-Fi, and its own Wi-Fi goes away.
    • Join your phone back to your home Wi-Fi.
    • Come back to mooboard.co/hi.
    • Setup carries on there. mooboard.local also works.

    Wi-Fi help

    Tips

    • mooboard needs a 2.4 GHz network. Most routers broadcast one under the same name as the rest. It may also be its own, like Home_2.4.
    • It joins WPA2 and WPA3 Personal networks, and open ones. Not networks that ask for a username.
    • Do not pick a guest network. Wi-Fi with a sign-in page, like at hotels, schools and work, won’t work.
    • A VPN or iCloud Private Relay can hide that you are home. Turn it off for your home Wi-Fi, so mooboard.co/hi can find the board.
    • Can’t see your network? Move the board closer to your router and scan again.
    • A hidden network? Tap Other network and type its name.

    Change Wi-Fi later

    • In the web app, open System, then Board. On Wi-Fi Status, tap Change Wi-Fi.
    • Pick the network, type its password and tap Save and restart.
    • Then join your phone to your home Wi-Fi and open mooboard.co/portal.
    • If the new network fails, the board shows its code again after 2 minutes.

    Factory reset

    • Hold BOOT for 10 seconds.
    • A fill sweeps across the screen from amber to red with Factory resetting…
    • At 10 seconds the words flash. Then the board restarts and shows its code to join.
    • It erases Wi-Fi, settings and sign-ins. Its own Wi-Fi gets a new password.
    • BOOT does nothing while the board shows its code to join.
    • Or in the web app, open System, then Advanced. Under Erase everything, type RESET and tap Erase.
