mooboard and Google

mooboard is an LED display for your wall. It shows the time, the weather, what’s playing, and your calendar and tasks.

What mooboard does

  • mooboard is a 128 by 32 pixel LED board that hangs on your wall.
  • It shows a clock, the weather, the song that’s playing with its lyrics, your next events and your open tasks.
  • You set it up from its web app on your phone or computer.

How mooboard uses your Google account

You can connect your Google account in your board’s web app. Your board then shows your upcoming Google Calendar events and your Google Tasks on its display.

mooboard asks Google for read-only access to three things:

  • Your calendar list, so you can pick which calendars show on your board.
  • The events on those calendars, so your board can show what’s next.
  • Your tasks, so your board can show what’s still open.

mooboard never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account. Your calendars and tasks go straight from Google to your board. They never come to us.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect Google at any time in your board’s web app, or in your Google Account’s third-party connections. A factory reset also erases the sign-in saved on your board.
